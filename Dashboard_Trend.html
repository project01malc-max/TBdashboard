🚨 Major Gaps Identified

  • ⚠️
    HIV/TB Treatment Linkage
    In 2026 (Jan–Jun), 3 TB patients tested positive for HIV with 2 initiated on ART (66.7% linkage). While improved from 2025 (6.1%), 100% immediate linkage must be maintained.
  • 🧬
    Universal DST Coverage
    In 2026 H1, 1,790 patients underwent DST out of 6,466 registered cases (27.7% coverage), indicating a continued need to scale rapid molecular drug susceptibility testing.
  • 📉
    Contact Tracing & Yield Optimization
    In 2026 H1, 7,977 contacts were screened yielding 353 diagnosed cases (4.4% yield), mirroring 2025 yield (4.4%) but down from 20.1% in 2023.

✅ Improvements Observed

  • 📈
    Strong Notification Pace in 2026
    Total case notifications reached 6,466 in H1 2026 (on pace for ~12.9k annual), bringing cumulative program registrations across 2023–2026 to 47,460 TB cases.
  • 🚀
    Sustained High TPT Initiation
    Preventive therapy initiation reached 605 contacts in H1 2026 (primarily 3HP: 458 and 6H: 97), continuing the major TPT programmatic scale-up.
  • 🎗️
    High-Volume HIV Screening Maintained
    In just 6 months of 2026, 2,186 TB patients were tested for HIV, matching the entire 12-month total of 2025!
  • 🔬
    Massive Diagnostic Reach
    OPD consultations reached 1.32 million and presumptive TB identification reached 28,282 in H1 2026 alone, with cumulative OPD surpassing 6.47 million.

📋 Executive Summary

The TB-07 trend analysis demonstrates robust programmatic expansion across 2023–2026 across all core performance indicators. Cumulative case notifications across the 4-year period reached 47,460 registered TB patients (12,841 in 2023, 13,340 in 2024, 14,813 in 2025, and 6,466 in Jan–Jun 2026). Total outpatient consultations over the period exceeded 6.47 million, with over 206,000 presumptive TB suspects identified.


Key programmatic achievements include an extraordinary expansion in TB Preventive Therapy (TPT) (from 79 in 2023 to 2,131 in 2025 and 605 in H1 2026), coupled with a dramatic surge in HIV testing among TB patients (over 5,600 patients screened). Key operational priorities for the second half of 2026 include: (1) Achieving 100% ART initiation for all HIV-positive TB patients, (2) Expanding universal rapid DST coverage beyond 30%, and (3) Expanding high-yield household contact investigation and symptom screening.
