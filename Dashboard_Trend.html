🚨 Major Gaps Identified

  • ⚠️
    HIV/TB Treatment Linkage Gap
    In 2025, while 33 TB patients tested positive for HIV, only 2 were initiated on ART, indicating a critical gap in care linkage.
  • 🧬
    Routine DST Screening Coverage
    Only 4,208 patients out of 14,813 registered cases underwent DST in 2025 (28.4% coverage), leaving potential drug resistance undetected.
  • 📉
    Declining Yield in Contact Tracing
    Contact screening expanded to 21,486 contacts in 2025, but yield dropped to 946 diagnosed TB cases (4.4% yield vs 20.1% in 2023).

✅ Improvements Observed

  • 📈
    Consistent Case Notification Growth
    Total case notifications increased from 12,841 (2023) to 13,340 (2024) and 14,813 (2025) — an overall +15.4% expansion.
  • 🚀
    Exponential Scale-Up in TPT
    Preventive therapy initiation scaled up dramatically from 79 (2023) to 694 (2024) and 2,131 (2025) household contacts.
  • 🎗️
    HIV Screening Expansion
    HIV screening among TB patients surged by +244.3% in 2025 (2,186 tested vs 635 in 2024).
  • 🔬
    OPD & Presumptive Case Finding
    OPD attendance expanded to 2.91 million and presumptive case identification grew to 65,788 in 2025 (+20.2% vs 2023).

📋 Executive Summary

The TB-07 trend analysis demonstrates sustained program growth from 2023 to 2025 across all core performance indicators. Consolidated total TB case notifications expanded from 12,841 cases in 2023 to 13,340 in 2024 (+3.9%) and reached 14,813 cases in 2025 (+11.0%), reflecting an overall 2-year growth of +15.4%.


Key programmatic achievements include a dramatic scale-up of TB Preventive Therapy (TPT) from 79 contacts in 2023 to 2,131 in 2025, alongside a surging HIV screening rate (2,186 tested in 2025). High-priority focus areas going forward include: (1) Strengthening ART initiation for HIV-positive TB patients, (2) Expanding routine DST coverage beyond 28%, and (3) Maintaining high-yield household contact investigation.
